# Website photography

All rendered photographs are bundled locally. Source references, native dimensions and checksums for selected originals are recorded in `IMAGE-SOURCES.json`. `IMAGE-PLACEMENTS.json` records the 38 editorial placements verified in the production build, including all hero scenes and all six atelier tabs. Each photograph belongs to one placement across the site; future replacements should preserve that rule.

## Ingredient and application photography

Original ingredient photographs and their responsive WebP variants remain in `public/images`. The selected dairy board is used only for the Dairy Ingredients category. The sauces photograph belongs only to the Culinary & sauces application, and the connected globe belongs only to Global Network. The original 4740 × 3121 dried fruit assortment belongs only to the Company sourcing story. These images illustrate ingredients and applications.

## Distinct editorial placements

Reference photography in `public/images/editorial` supplies separate photographs for Home collections, Home supporting sections, the beverage and next-creation gallery tabs, and the Company hero. Previously remote coconut, berry and dairy application photographs are also stored there for reliable loading. Source files are retained without enlargement or recompression.

Food Colours uses the purple, green and yellow powder bowls formerly shown in the Home hero. The Home colour scene now has its own green citrus-drink photograph. Spray-Dried Powders uses a separate source-fruit photograph. The fruit tart, milk-pouring, lime-tree and hibiscus photographs each have a single dedicated gallery placement.
